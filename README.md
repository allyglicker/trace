# Trace

Point your phone at paper, drop a faint photo on top, and draw what you see.

**Use it:** https://allyglicker.github.io/trace/

## How it works

1. Open the link on your phone and tap **Turn on camera**.
2. Tap **Photo** to load the image you want to trace.
3. Drag to move it, pinch to resize and rotate. Use **Fade** to adjust transparency.
4. Tap **Outline** to turn the photo into line art; **Detail** controls how many lines show.
5. Prop the phone on a stand and tap **Lock** so stray touches don't move the image.

Everything runs in the browser; photos never leave your device.

## Notes

The camera only works over `https://`, so open the hosted link above rather than a downloaded copy of the file.
